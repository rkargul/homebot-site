homebot

A private assistant for one household

homebot is a personal Telegram assistant that its owner runs on a computer at home, for a single household. It is not a public service: it only answers Telegram accounts on its owner's allowlist and ignores everyone else.

It answers questions with a language model that runs on that home computer, sends a morning briefing (weather, the day's calendar and news headlines), and, with a user's permission, reads their Google Calendar and adds events they ask for. Every new event is shown first and only created after the user taps Add.
