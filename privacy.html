Privacy policy

homebot · effective 18 October 2026

homebot is a private Telegram assistant that its owner runs on a computer at home, for a single household (about homebot). Only Telegram accounts on its allowlist can use it. This policy explains what it does with your data, and in particular with data from your Google account.

Google user data homebot accesses

Only if you connect Google Calendar (with /calendar connect), and only with these permissions:

  • Your list of calendars (calendar.calendarlist.readonly): names and IDs, so you can choose which calendars appear in your morning briefing and which one new events go to.
  • Events in your calendars (calendar.events): homebot reads the current day's events for your morning briefing. It creates an event only after showing it to you and you tapping Add, and it deletes an event only when you tap Undo on one it created.

homebot cannot change calendar sharing or settings, and cannot delete calendars.

How the data is used

  • Only to show you your own calendar in your own Telegram chat and to add the events you confirm.
  • Your messages and calendar details are processed by a language model that runs on the home computer. They are not sent to any AI service.
  • Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train AI or machine-learning models, and never shared with anyone else. The only exception is Telegram, which delivers homebot's messages to you. Nobody else reads it, apart from the owner as the operator of the computer it runs on.

homebot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data is stored, and for how long

  • The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the home computer, in a local database and its local backups, until you disconnect or Google revokes it.
  • Your chat history with homebot, including morning briefings and confirmation cards, is kept on that computer for 30 days and then deleted. Local backups are kept for 7 days.
  • Messages pass through Telegram to reach you. Telegram's privacy policy applies to them.

Your choices

  • /calendar disconnect in the chat revokes homebot's access to your Google account immediately. You can also remove it at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • /forget deletes your chat history, and optionally your notes, from the home computer.
